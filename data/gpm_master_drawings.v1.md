## Interpretation

- Standard titles and aliases describe catalog entries; keywords are search hints, not unique identifiers.
- Drawing number is the document identifier resolved from the spreadsheet hyperlink. It is not necessarily the component's part number.
- Link text preserves the spreadsheet's displayed label. Link targets are copied exactly, including `*` and `%2a`.
- Multiple entries can share a drawing. A title can also lead to several different drawings; do not silently choose between those destinations.
- `XXX` in a title is source text, not an instruction to invent an identifier.
- An entry does not establish a specific revision. These source URLs use `latestreleased`.
- This catalog does not establish whether a link is currently accessible or whether the caller is authorized to open it.
